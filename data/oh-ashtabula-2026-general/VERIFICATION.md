## Election Dates & Procedures

### Key Dates
- **Election Day:** Tuesday, November 3, 2026 (polls 6:30am-7:30pm)
- **Early in-person voting:** October 6 - November 1, 2026
- **Absentee application deadline:** October 27, 2026 by 8:30pm (must be RECEIVED)
- **Absentee return deadline:** Must be RECEIVED by 7:30pm November 3, 2026
- **Voter registration deadline:** October 5, 2026 (passed)

### Critical Rule Change
- **Ohio SB 293 (effective 2026):** Eliminated the postmark grace period. Mailed absentee ballots must be physically RECEIVED by the BOE by 7:30pm Election Day. Postmarks do not count.
- **Source:** Referenced in ballot.md; Ohio Revised Code 3509.05
