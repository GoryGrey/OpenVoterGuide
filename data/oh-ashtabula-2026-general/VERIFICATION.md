# Ashtabula County General Election Guide: Sources and Audit Status

Last reviewed: October 11, 2026.

## Official election sources

- [Ashtabula County Board of Elections](https://www.boe.ohio.gov/ashtabula/) identifies the November 3, 2026 General Election, links the official candidate list, issue list, and sample ballots, and publishes the county office address and phone number.
- [Ohio Secretary of State 2026 voting schedule](https://www.ohiosos.gov/elections/voting-schedule-text-only) confirms Election Day, early voting dates and hours, registration and absentee request deadlines, and that non-UOCAVA absentee ballots must reach the board by 7:30 p.m. on Election Day.
- [Ohio Revised Code § 3509.05](https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-3509.05), effective March 20, 2026 under SB 293, sets the return deadline and who may deliver a mailed absentee ballot. UOCAVA voters have a separate deadline under § 3511.11.

The county's candidate-list PDF is revised September 15, 2026. The BOE file named Election_Issues.pdf currently identifies the May 5, 2026 Primary Election, so it is not a valid source for November local issues. This review now uses the county auditor's November levy estimator, the Ohio Secretary of State's November Local Questions and Issues workbook, and the BOE sample ballot selector. The county ballot data page reports a September 11, 2026 update.

## Coverage and counts

This guide currently includes 20 race pages with 45 named candidates, including the seven write-in candidates named in the official candidate list; 2 measure pages (Issues 3 and 10); 36 property-tax levies; and 3 precinct-specific liquor questions (Issues 41–43). The 9 additional race pages cover two 11th District Court of Appeals seats, four Common Pleas seats, County Commissioner, County Auditor, and County Coroner. Several new profiles intentionally contain only official ballot-list information until more candidate details can be independently sourced. Issue 10 is a Conneaut charter amendment, not a property-tax levy.

The county auditor's November estimator lists all 36 levy numbers, jurisdictions, purposes, millage rates, and categories. Its roster matches the guide's 36 issue numbers: 25 straight renewals, 7 additional levies, 3 renewals with increases, and 1 renewal with a reduction. This review does not treat the estimator as proof of every duration, dollar estimate, or ballot sentence. Check those on the selected precinct ballot and the Secretary of State workbook.

The countywide BOE sample-ballot selector lists November 3, 2026 ballot styles. The voter-specific ballot selector and the Secretary of State workbook are the current sources for local questions. The BOE PDF previously cited for local issues is a May primary document and must not be used to certify November questions. Issue 10 is treated separately from the tax levies as a Conneaut charter amendment.

## Candidate information: scope of verification

Candidate names and ballot presence were checked against the county's official candidate list, revised September 15, 2026. Named write-in candidates are included for U.S. Senator and Governor/Lieutenant Governor. Candidate profiles use the party wording from that list where applicable. Court of Common Pleas candidates are shown without party labels because Ohio law requires those contests on the nonpartisan ballot; the candidate list's primary-party headings are not ballot labels. See the Ohio Secretary of State Election Official Manual, Chapter 5.

This verifies candidate names and ballot presence, not every biography, campaign position, voting-history item, or comparison. Claim-level source audits are complete for Ohio House Districts 65 and 99, the listed county and court profiles, and Ohio’s 14th Congressional District. In OH-14, Joyce’s biography and recorded votes link to official House sources; Jukic’s policy positions are linked as campaign statements, and a complete individual Euclid City Council vote history remains pending. Jon Husted’s U.S. Senate profile was reviewed against his Senate biography, official roll calls, and bill text for six selected statements and six selected actions. Sherrod Brown’s profile was reviewed against his campaign platform, official House and Senate records, and Congress.gov for four selected campaign statements and seven selected actions. These are limited claim-level audits, not comprehensive platform or voting-history reviews; neither comparison assigns a match score. The Governor and Lieutenant Governor profiles for Vivek Ramaswamy / Robert McColley and Amy Acton / David Pepper were reviewed against campaign biographies and platforms and Ohio Senate roll calls for selected statements and actions. William B. Redpath’s and Greg Levy’s U.S. Senate profiles were reviewed against their campaign platforms for selected statements and background. These are limited claim-level audits. U.S. Senate write-in ticket profiles and other statewide and multicounty candidate claims still need review. A source link establishes what a source says or records; it does not independently validate campaign claims, predictions, or this guide’s interpretation.

## Nonpartisanship

The guide does not endorse candidates or ballot outcomes. It presents descriptions and links so readers can check primary documents and decide for themselves.
